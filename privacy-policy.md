# Astra Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 7, 2026**

Astra is a podcast player developed and published by threemfs ("Astra," "we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how Astra handles information when you use the Android app.


## Summary

Astra is designed to work primarily from data stored on your device. It does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs, and it does not sell personal information.

Astra sends information off your device only when needed to provide a feature you use, such as searching for podcasts, loading a podcast's RSS feed or media, or using the optional Google Drive backup feature.

## Information Astra stores on your device

Astra stores the following information locally on your device to provide podcast playback and library features:

- Podcast subscriptions, RSS feed URLs, podcast and episode metadata, artwork references, and library order.
- Playback information, including the current episode, playback position, played status, manual queue, and listening preferences.
- App and per-podcast settings, such as playback speed, skip intervals, silence trimming, theme, episode sort order, and Smart Queue preferences.
- The location you select for downloaded episodes and references to downloaded audio files.
- Locally cached artwork and RSS data needed to display and play podcasts.

This information is not sent to Astra-operated servers because Astra does not operate an account system or a central Astra server.

## Network services and information sent to them

### Podcast discovery

When you search for podcasts or use popular-podcast discovery, Astra connects to the following services:

- **Podcast Index** to search its directory and retrieve podcast metadata. Search terms and the network information normally accompanying an internet request, such as your IP address and device/app user-agent, are sent to Podcast Index.
- **Apple Marketing Tools RSS Feed** to retrieve the public U.S. podcast chart used to order popular-podcast results. Standard network information, such as your IP address and app user-agent, is sent with that request.

These services process requests under their own privacy policies.

### Podcast publishers and hosting providers

When you open, refresh, stream, or download a podcast, Astra connects directly to the relevant podcast's RSS feed, artwork host, and audio host. Those providers receive the information normally included in a direct internet request, which can include your IP address, request headers, and the requested feed, artwork, or audio URL.

Astra does not control those third-party services or their privacy practices.

### Optional Google Drive backup

Google Drive backup is optional and is off until you choose to connect a Google account and enable it in Astra's Settings.

If you enable it, Astra requests the Google Drive `drive.appdata` scope. This limited scope allows Astra to create, read, and update one private backup file in your Google Drive app-data folder. That folder is not visible in your normal Google Drive file list and is accessible to Astra through the authorization you grant.

The backup can contain:

- Your subscribed podcast feed URLs and library order.
- Episode identifiers, audio URLs, played status, and saved playback positions for episodes with listening progress.
- Global and per-podcast playback and queue preferences.

It does **not** include downloaded audio files, cached feed descriptions, cached artwork, silence-time totals, or a copy of the currently open playback session.

Astra uses a short-lived Google authorization token supplied by Google Play services to access this file. Astra does not store your Google password or a long-lived Google access token. You can disable Google Drive backup in Astra at any time. You can delete the private backup by removing Astra's app data from your Google account or by using a future in-app delete option when available.

### Android device backup and device transfer

Astra currently permits Android's system backup and device-transfer mechanisms. If you have enabled Android device backup, Android or your chosen device-backup provider may back up Astra app data according to your device settings and that provider's policies. This may include locally stored Astra library data and preferences. You can control Android device backup through your device settings.

## Information Astra does not collect or use

Astra does not:

- Require or create an Astra account.
- Collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, camera, microphone, or advertising ID.
- Use advertising, behavioral advertising, or sell or rent personal information.
- Include third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.
- Upload your downloaded podcast audio to Astra or to Google Drive through the optional Astra backup feature.

## Android Auto and media controls

Astra exposes podcast playback information to Android system media controls and, when connected, Android Auto. This lets those systems display currently playing media and send standard playback commands. Astra does not receive vehicle telemetry, location, or driving data from Android Auto.

## Data retention and deletion

Most Astra data remains on your device until you remove it, uninstall Astra, clear Astra's app storage, unsubscribe from a podcast, or delete a downloaded episode.

If you use Google Drive backup, the backup remains in your private Google Drive app-data folder until it is replaced, removed through your Google account, or deleted using an Astra feature that supports deletion. Disabling automatic backup stops future Astra backups but does not itself delete an existing backup.

Because Astra does not maintain Astra accounts or Astra-hosted user databases, there is no Astra account to delete. Your Google account and any Google Drive backup are governed by Google and can be managed through your Google account settings.

## Security

Astra uses HTTPS for its connections to Podcast Index, Apple Marketing Tools, Google Drive, and HTTPS podcast services. No internet transmission method or storage system is completely secure, but Astra limits data transmission to what is needed for the requested feature and uses the platform's standard security mechanisms for Google authorization.

## Children

Astra is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Astra's features or data practices change. We will post the updated policy with a new effective date. Material changes to data handling will also be reflected in the app and Google Play disclosures where required.
